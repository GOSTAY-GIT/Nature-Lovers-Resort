🌿 About Kavish Jawai Village Resort

Kavish Jawai Village Resort is a boutique luxury wildlife resort nestled in the picturesque Jawai Perwa, Rajasthan. The resort offers 10 well-appointed garden-facing villas equipped with modern amenities, providing guests with a serene and comfortable stay amidst nature. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

Ideal for weekend getaways, leisure trips, and intimate weddings, the resort features a garden lawn perfect for small gatherings and celebrations. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

🏡 Accommodation Types

  • Garden-Facing Villas — Luxurious villas offering views of the lush gardens.
  • Luxury Tents — Glamping-style tents combining comfort with nature.

🌟 Unique Experiences & Activities

  • Leopard Safari — Guided safaris to spot leopards in their natural habitat.
  • Bird Watching — Explore the diverse avian life around the resort.
  • Crocodile Sighting — Witness crocodiles in their natural environment.
  • Nature Walks — Guided walks through the scenic landscapes of Jawai.
  • Shiv Temple Visit — Explore the nearby Shiv Temple, just 2.5 km from the resort.
  • Jawai Dam — Visit the Jawai Dam, located 30 km away, for a day trip.

🎉 Events & Corporate

  • Destination Weddings — Scenic backdrops and event spaces for memorable weddings.
  • Corporate Retreats & Group Bookings — Team activities and offsite meeting options.

🚙 How to Reach

  • Nearest Railway Station: Jawai Bandh (~15 km)
  • Nearest Airports: Udaipur (~90 km)
  • The resort is accessible by road from nearby towns — best to confirm pickup/transfer options with the resort.

📸 Why Stay Here / Highlights

  • Boutique luxury amidst the wilderness of Jawai.
  • Proximity to the Jawai Leopard Conservation Zone.
  • Ideal for wildlife enthusiasts and nature lovers.
  • Offers a blend of modern comforts and rustic charm.
