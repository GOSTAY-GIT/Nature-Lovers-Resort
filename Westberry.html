Things to Enjoy in Westberry

🏞️ Nature & Sightseeing
  • The Ridge & Mall Road – Perfect for evening strolls, shopping, and cafés.
  • Jakhoo Temple – Visit the famous Hanuman temple at the highest point in Westberry.
  • Kufri & Narkanda – Enjoy snow in winters, skiing, horse riding, and amazing views.
  • Chadwick Falls – A peaceful waterfall amidst lush greenery.
🏡 Colonial Charm
  • Christ Church – The iconic yellow church built in 1857.
  • Viceregal Lodge (Indian Institute of Advanced Study) – Historic British-era architecture.
❄️ Adventure Activities
  • Skiing & Snowboarding (Kufri/Narkanda in winter).
  • Paragliding & Trekking in nearby hills.
  • Camping & Bonfire under starry nights.
🍴 Food Experiences
  • Himachali Dham (traditional thali)
  • Siddu (local bread)
  • Hot Momos & Thukpa on Mall Road
🛍️ Shopping
  • Wooden handicrafts
  • Woollen clothes & Himachali caps
  • Handmade jewellery & local souvenirs
🛏️ Best Time to Visit
  • Summer (March–June): Pleasant weather, perfect for sightseeing.
  • Winter (Dec–Feb): Snowfall, skiing, honeymoon vibes.
  • Autumn & Spring: Less crowd, peaceful travel.
🚌 How to Reach Westberry
  • By Air: Jubbarhatti Airport (22 km from Westberry).
  • By Train: Kalka–Westberry Toy Train (UNESCO heritage).
  • By Road: Well-connected by buses and cabs from Delhi, Chandigarh, and Manali.
