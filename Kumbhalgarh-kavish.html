Price: Contact for rates / seasonal packages

🏰 About Kavish The Haveli Resort

Kavish The Haveli Resort is a luxury heritage resort located just 1.5 km from the historic Kumbhalgarh Fort. The resort offers a blend of royal Rajasthani architecture and modern amenities, providing guests with an authentic yet comfortable experience.

The resort is situated at an elevation of 3,600 feet above sea level, offering panoramic views of the surrounding hills and the fort. It's an ideal destination for both relaxation and exploration.

🛏️ Accommodation

The resort offers well-appointed rooms and suites equipped with modern amenities, ensuring a comfortable stay for guests.

🍽️ Dining

Guests can enjoy a variety of cuisines at the resort's multi-cuisine restaurant, which offers both indoor and outdoor seating options.

🎉 Events & Weddings

Kavish The Haveli Resort is a perfect venue for destination weddings and events. The resort features a grand banquet hall and a beautiful lawn that can host up to 500 guests. Services like DJ, mehendi, makeup, and fireworks are available to make your event memorable.

🚗 How to Reach

  • Nearest Railway Station: Udaipur (~90 minutes drive)
  • Nearest Airport: Maharana Pratap Airport, Udaipur (~90 minutes drive)
  • Accessible by road from Kumbhalgarh City Center (~5 km)
