🌳 About Denwa Winds Resort

Denwa Winds Resort is spread over about 12 acres beside the river Denwa, tucked into mango groves and the jungles around Pachmarhi / Satpura. The property blends local Satpura culture with a nature-first design to give guests a tranquil, immersive wilderness experience.

Expect river/forest views, natural sounds, open dining and plenty of opportunities for birding, walks and wildlife photography. The resort positions itself as a peaceful retreat close to nature while offering comfortable amenities.

🏘️ Accommodation Types

  • Denwa View Cottages — cottages with views of the Denwa river and surrounding forest
  • Family Cluster Cottages — grouped cottages ideal for families or small groups
  • Lakeside Swiss Tents — lake-side glamping style tents for a rustic stay
  • The White House — larger/private option for guests seeking exclusivity

🌿 Unique Experiences & Activities

  • Swim in Nature — natural/outsourced swimming experiences (check with resort for exact details)
  • Open Dining — meals served with local flavours and scenic views
  • Lakeside Walks & Jaltarang Lake — calm lakeside areas for strolls and relaxation
  • Rain Dance & Recreational Activities — events and fun activities for groups
  • Gym & Games — basic recreational facilities available on site
  • Wildlife & Bird Watching — great spot for photography and birding excursions

🎉 Events & Corporate

  • Destination Weddings — scenic backdrops and event spaces
  • Corporate Retreats & Group Bookings — team activities and offsite meeting options

🚙 How to Reach

  • Nearest Railway Station: Pipariya (~20 km)
  • Nearest Airports: Bhopal (~165 km) and Nagpur (~230 km)
  • Resort is accessible by road from Pachmarhi and surrounding towns — best to confirm pickup/transfer options with the resort.

📸 Why Stay Here / Highlights

  • River-side setting in a mango grove — peaceful, scenic views
  • Blend of local Satpura culture and nature-forward design
  • Good for birdwatching, photography, family stays and small events
  • Several accommodation styles (cottages, tents, private house)
